PERSONAL STATEMENT WRITING GUIDE AND ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE
Version: 27 October 2026

PURPOSE
Write an accurate, personal explanation of your study decision. This is a planning guide, not a statement to copy, a university word-limit rule or a visa guarantee. Follow your chosen university’s instructions and length limit. Aim for specific evidence rather than impressive-sounding general claims.

1. INTRODUCE YOURSELF
Explain your relevant education, employment, achievements and interests. Check names, dates, grades and job responsibilities against your actual records. Explain changes in academic direction and study gaps honestly.

2. WHY THIS COURSE?
Explain the experience, problem or ambition that led you to this subject. Describe how you researched it before deciding. Use the official page for your actual programme, campus and intake. Choose three real modules, explain what each covers in your own words, and connect each to a skill you need, personal development and future work. Do not invent module names or assume a similar course has the same content.

3. WHY THIS UNIVERSITY?
Compare your chosen institution with at least three genuine alternatives. Record the course names, official URLs and research dates. Compare specific modules, assessment, facilities, professional links and costs where relevant. Explain why the selected combination fits you better. Avoid unsupported rankings or claims that another university is inferior.

4. WHY THIS DESTINATION?
Explain your preferred country in relation to your actual programme. Compare it with Nigeria or Ghana, as applicable, and three other destinations you researched. Consider academic structure, course modules, institutions, learning environment, duration and realistic living arrangements. Support factual claims with official sources. Do not rely on stereotypes or dismiss your home country’s education system.

5. FUTURE PLANS AND GENUINE TIES
Explain short- and long-term goals, relevant industries or employers and the work you intend to do. If discussing an existing or planned business, explain your own role and a realistic plan. Discuss genuine family, professional or community connections where they are relevant. Do not invent dependants, property, jobs or promises of return. Explain your real intentions, including any lawful post-study options, truthfully. Lack of family ties alone is not proof of an unsuitable applicant.

BEFORE SHARING WITH STAFF
- Verify every module, fee, date, qualification and personal claim.
- Include source links and research dates in your working draft.
- Use your own words. Do not submit this sample unchanged.
- Disclose previous visa refusals accurately where asked.
- Remove unnecessary bank-account numbers or sensitive identifiers.
- Ask staff to review unsupported claims and inconsistencies.

CRITICAL-THINKING PRACTICE
If your favourite module were unavailable, would the course still fit? Why?
What evidence might change your choice of university?
Which alternative destination is strongest for your goal, and what trade-off did you accept?
How would you adapt if your first career plan did not happen immediately?
Explain one statement claim using a concrete example rather than repeating the wording.
