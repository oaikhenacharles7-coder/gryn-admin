PERSONAL STATEMENT AND CREDIBILITY PREPARATION GUIDE
Standard: gii-preparation-2026-11-21

Write your own truthful account. This guide is not a statement to copy, a visa prediction or a requirement to invent return plans, employment, research or family ties. Follow the university's actual instructions and word limit.

1. INTRODUCTION AND EDUCATION — 10 POINTS
Introduce yourself, your previous qualification, institution, dates and relevant academic interests. Check these against your CV and certificates.

2. CURRENT ACTIVITIES AND TIME SINCE SCHOOL — 15 POINTS
Explain what you do now and what you have done since graduation. Include actual employment, business, volunteering, further study or other relevant activities. Explain study gaps honestly and connect your experience to the proposed course where relevant.

3. COURSE CHOICE AND ACADEMIC PROGRESSION — 25 POINTS
Explain what motivates this course and how it progresses from or differs from your previous studies. Research the actual programme, campus and intake. Explain at least three real published modules in your own words: what each covers, why it matters to you and how you expect to use it. Use the appropriate undergraduate or postgraduate programme modules, not an unrelated master's course.

4. UNIVERSITY CHOICE AND LEARNING ENVIRONMENT — 20 POINTS
Explain what the university is known for in your subject and why its teaching, research, facilities, support and environment suit your learning. Verify claims through official sources. For deeper preparation, compare three genuine alternatives, including actual module differences. Do not invent rankings or dismiss another institution without evidence.

5. DESTINATION CHOICE — 15 POINTS
For the UK, explain why its relevant education and research opportunities fit your needs compared with options you researched in your home country. Use specific evidence rather than blanket claims that every UK institution is better. For another destination, adapt this comparison to that actual country. Explain alternatives you genuinely researched.

6. FUTURE CAREER AND CONTRIBUTION — 15 POINTS
Explain realistic short- and longer-term career goals and how the qualification supports them. Discuss your genuine intention to return and contribute to your home country, community or environment where that is your actual plan. Mention real employers, sectors, business plans or community goals where researched. Do not invent intentions or conceal lawful plans.

ILLUSTRATIVE WRITING FRAMEWORK — PERSONALISE EVERY PART

I completed [actual qualification] at [institution] in [year]. My interest in [subject] developed through [a real experience].

Since graduating, I have [truthful activities with dates]. In my role as [actual role], I encountered [a real challenge]. This helped me identify a need to develop [specific knowledge or skill].

I chose [exact programme] because [researched reasons]. The published modules [module one], [module two] and [module three] address [specific topics]. I expect to use this learning to [realistic personal and career application].

I selected [university/campus] after researching [genuine alternatives]. Its [verified subject strength or facility] and [learning environment] fit my needs because [personal explanation].

I selected [destination] after comparing [specific study and research opportunities] with options in [home country and genuinely considered alternatives]. My choice reflects [evidence-based reasons].

After study, my genuine plan is [actual plan]. I intend to use [specific skills] in [role, business, sector or community contribution]. If I plan to return home, my reasons and intended contribution are [truthful, specific details].

RESEARCH WORKSHEET
For each claim record: university/course, official URL, date checked, module/facility/fee information and why it matters to you. School start dates, tuition, deposits and living costs must be checked against the actual offer/CAS and current official guidance.

INTERVIEW PRACTICE
A standard mock draws 20 questions from the wider question bank. Prepare to explain education, current activities, course, university, destination, career, finances, accommodation, travel/refusals and agent involvement. Use your own words. Be ready for follow-up questions that test reasoning and trade-offs. The attachment-derived question topics are adapted training material, not a universal official UKVI script. Do not copy another applicant's answers.

GRADING
The six sections total 100 preparation points. A complete grade requires all six to have been assessed. Unasked or unverified topics remain ungraded. Grades measure the coverage, specificity and explanation supplied; they do not establish honesty, immigration intent or visa/admission outcomes. Assigned staff review AI feedback before acting.

FINANCIAL PREPARATION
Distinguish a funds-holding period from transaction-history evidence. Follow the current official rule for your country, route and funding method. Explain large deposits and source of funds. Do not double-count a tuition deposit as both an extra fee and part of the remaining tuition balance.

Bring your CV, offer/CAS, course research, own statement and relevant financial documents to preparation. Inform the participant and record consent before any recording or provider-based AI/document review.
